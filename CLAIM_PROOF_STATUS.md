# Claim and Proof Status

This document prevents check-count inflation from being mistaken for proof
of a universal definition. `verify_manuscript_numbers.py` verifies stored
claims against stored outputs; it does not turn canonical labels into
analytic truth.

## Evidence tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Examples | What it licenses |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Analytic ground truth | Boolean gates; exact Markov chains; algebraic option-value regions | Calibration of a named dimension |
| B | Constructed mechanism truth | common driver, central controller, pairwise chain, scripted/prewired controls | Falsification and known failure routes |
| C | Canonical convergent validity | Kuramoto, Boids, Schelling, Life, ant trail | Agreement with established usage, not proof of definition |
| D | External empirical evidence | Overcooked, chess, Pythia, grokking/induction-head behavior | Transfer/predictive evidence under declared observer contracts |
| E | Independent replication | third-party rerun, independent annotations/implementation | Not yet complete |

## Claims that are formally established

1. **Trajectory/readout identities.** Average specific collapse equals
   mutual information; deterministic readout collapse equals surprisal;
   coarse basin divergences lower-bound trajectory divergences
   (Proposition 0).
2. **Representation-jump bound.** A representation jump is bounded by
   stepwise distributional change via total variation/Pinsker
   (Proposition 1).
3. **Outcome revelation is insufficient.** Under a matched same-time
   mechanism null, `P_real=P_null` implies `C=G=0` even when the posterior
   over a final result becomes deterministic (revelation-null theorem).
4. **Observational non-identifiability.** Common-cause synergy and
   endogenous role-lock can have identical joint distributions; no
   observational statistic separates them. Environment conditioning or
   intervention is necessary in that construction.
5. **Pairwise reducibility.** `KL(P || P^(2))=0` for the exact pairwise
   family used in the irreducibility calibration.
6. **Magnitude is not strength/value.** Matched-collapse constructions
   differ in irreducibility and signed value.

## Claims supported empirically, not proved universally

1. Frozen coordinates transfer to the audited held-out families.
2. Canonical examples are compatible with the possibility-space ontology.
3. The adaptive certificate rejects the audited scripts, clones, twins and
   metric artefacts on their predicted routes.
4. Collapse profiles accompany audited grokking and induction-head
   capability formation and reject the audited memorization controls.
5. Stored Overcooked role statistics support a real-system bridge.

## Claims not yet established

1. **Necessity across all emergence.** It is not proved that every use of
   “emergence” in physics, biology, cognition and AI has nonzero
   mechanism-attributable possibility transformation under one admissible
   contract.
2. **Sufficiency.** Passing the current certificate is not proved sufficient
   for every scientifically legitimate notion of emergence.
3. **One estimator across domains.** The canonical matrix currently shares
   an ontology/schema but uses domain-specific estimators.
4. **Observer uniqueness.** `psi`, perturbation ensemble `nu`, horizon and
   null mechanism are declared/audited, not uniquely determined by nature.
5. **Observational identification.** Without intervention or a justified
   causal graph, common cause and endogenous interaction may be
   indistinguishable.
6. **High-dimensional consistency/scalability.** Exact connected
   information and joint future laws do not yet scale to large agent counts
   or unrestricted continuous trajectories.
7. **Full real-system mechanism cut.** The stored Overcooked checkout lacks
   policy checkpoints and trajectories for true agent-channel replay.
8. **CE 2.0 completion.** Multi-scale causal-contribution ladders and
   emergent-complexity spectra are not yet implemented.
9. **Temporal foreshadow certificate.** Non-additive ordered contribution of
   separated historical events (`T_syn`) remains incomplete.
10. **Independent replication.** Most experiments and observer contracts
    were implemented by the same project.

## Current defensible claim

> We provide a falsifiable, observer-declared interventional framework for
> testing whether an apparent macro organization or capability is
> accompanied by endogenous, selective and stable reorganization of a
> reachable possibility space. Analytic calibration, mechanism controls,
> canonical convergent validity and external machine-learning evidence
> support the framework across the audited families.

## Current indefensible claim

> We have proved that all emergence in nature is possibility collapse, or
> that the 105 consistency checks constitute 105 independent confirmations
> of that universal proposition.
