### `fig13_representation_jump_bridge.png`

Shows a controlled bridge between possibility collapse and representation-space
macro jumps. The experiment defines:

```text
C_t = KL(P_t(B) || P_0(B))
B_t = max(C_t - C_{t-1}, 0)
R_t = E_{B ~ P_t}[phi(B)]
J_t = ||R_t - R_{t-1}||_2
```

The strong-emergence regime has the largest collapse burst, largest
representation jump, and strongest burst-jump correlation.

Use in paper:

- connects the proposed mechanism to the existing representation-jump view;
- reframes representation jumps as observable consequences of possibility
  collapse;
- answers the reviewer concern that possibility collapse is disconnected from
  familiar emergence metrics.

Limitation:

- controlled basin projection, not yet learned neural embeddings.

### `fig33_chess_collapse.png`

Within-state useful collapse in a real strategic system: 240 externally
annotated sacrificial key moves (lichess puzzle database, real rated
human games) plus 120 balanced quiet middlegame controls, measured with
Stockfish 14.1 under an imperfect-play observer (protocol frozen in
`CHESS_PREREGISTRATION.md` before measurement). Left: future win-basin
probability under forced key move versus deep alternative, shallow
greedy, random, and quiet-control best moves. Middle: the key move's
material cost against best reply versus the win probability it buys.
Right: per-position do-contrast distributions for sacrifice versus quiet
positions.

Key readings:

- the annotated key move is genuinely locally costly (median -3.0 pawns
  against best reply, 80% strictly costly) yet forcing it reaches the
  win basin at 0.78 versus 0.24 for the best deep alternative -- gap
  0.54, sign test 240/240, p = 5.7e-73 (C1-C3 registered passes);
- median potential before the key move is 1.19 bits under the frozen
  observer: the win is present but not inevitable (C4 pass);
- quiet balanced positions have HIGHER potential (1.93 bits) but no
  move that usefully collapses it (best-move do-contrast ~0): collapse
  opportunities are properties of (state, action) structure, not of
  the game per se.

Limitations (registered):

- C5's frozen 0.25 effect-size margin FAILED via the route recorded
  before the main run: the observer plays the key move endogenously, so
  the base P(win) already contains it and the shift-vs-base statistic
  is compressed (0.057). The do-contrast (C3) is the right measurement;
  the failure is reported in the registration outcome section;
- one engine family (Stockfish NNUE) supplies playouts and basin
  classification; depth-limited playouts are an imperfect-play model,
  not human play;
- puzzle themes are pipeline/community labels; the sacrifice tag is
  external to us but not hand-audited by experts move-by-move.

Within-episode collapse in a deep MARL benchmark (PettingZoo/MPE

(CE = best macro EI - micro EI), and Rosas' practical criterion
Psi = I(V_t; V_{t+1}) - sum_j I(X_j; V_{t+1}) maximized over four
supervenient features x two micro decompositions. Blue bars = truly
emergent systems, gray = imitations.

Key readings:

- exact CE is NEGATIVE for every system (micro EI 9.6-11.3 bits; no
  macro in the family beats it -- near-deterministic multi-agent
  chains leave no room for macro-beats-micro), and the hindsight-best
  threshold in either direction is the trivial all-negative
  classifier (acc 0.8, missing both true positives); even the
  ordering fails (blind_trigger below latent_conditional);
- exact Psi with the theory's own verdict (Psi > 0) scores 0.3: six
  false positives, top scorer is wrong_selector (+0.59, triggers in
  exactly the wrong mode), and noise_policy missed; the hindsight
  optimum (0.9) requires INVERTING the published sign convention and
  still misses latent_conditional;
- both published-form audits perform at or below the charitable proxies, so the
  comparison's blind-spot conclusion is structural (no access to value
  sign, natural selectivity, or provenance), not an artifact of proxy
  simplification. This is the measured half of the coverage theorem
  (Prop. 5, THEORY.md).

Limitations:

- Hoel's framework quantifies over ALL coarse-grainings; exhaustive
  search is super-exponential (not performed in the original papers
  either), so CE here is a lower bound over a structured five-member
  family that includes the mechanistically privileged partitions --
  the trivial-classifier conclusion holds for this family, stated as
  such;
- Psi is the first-order practical criterion (redundancy correction
  dropped, as in Rosas et al.'s own applications); since verdicts are
  also evaluated at a hindsight-optimal threshold, constant offsets
  cannot rescue the ordering failures;
- both quantities are computed on the gridworld battery only (the one
  domain where exact enumeration is feasible); on external domains
  the sampled proxies of fig34 remain the comparison.

Limitations:

- small grid (5x5, 2 agents, 2 foods); the do-gap median is modest
  because at T=6 probe temperature many pre-commit states already
  favor clearance;
- two pilot estimator fixes (probe temperature, do-release on
  consumption) were needed before the main run; both documented with
  logs and made before any main measurement.
