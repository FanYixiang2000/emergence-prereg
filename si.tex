\section*{Supplementary Methods}

\paragraph{Ant-colony commitment model.}
$N$ ants make repeated trips, each choosing between two routes $A$ and
$B$. Route pheromones $\varphi_A,\varphi_B$ are initialized at $1.0$;
on each trip every ant chooses $A$ with probability
$p=(K+\varphi_A)^{\alpha}/[(K+\varphi_A)^{\alpha}+(K+\varphi_B)^{\alpha}]$
with $K=5$ and $\alpha=2$ (the standard nonlinear pheromone choice
function), after which pheromones update as
$\varphi\leftarrow\varphi(1-\rho)+q\,n$ with evaporation $\rho=0.01$,
per-capita deposit $q=Q_{\mathrm{total}}/N$, total deposit budget
$Q_{\mathrm{total}}=0.5$ and $n$ the number of ants that chose the
route. Because the deposit budget is shared, the fluctuation scale is
$N^{-1/2}$, which is the source of the $\ln N$ commitment-time
prediction recorded before the finite-size study. Openness is the
binary entropy of the route-choice probability. The breakpoint study
uses sizes $\{1,10,100\}$ with 900 trips; the finite-size study uses
sizes $1$--$500$ with a 1{,}500-trip horizon, checkpoints every 10
trips, 30 episodes per size and 200 bootstrap resamples for the
log-law slope.

\paragraph{Kuramoto model.}
$N=200$ oscillators follow
$\dot{\theta}_i=\omega_i+Kr\sin(\psi-\theta_i)$ plus Gaussian noise of
scale $\sigma=0.05$, where $(r,\psi)$ is the instantaneous mean field,
natural frequencies are drawn from $\mathcal{N}(0,0.5^2)$, and
integration uses Euler--Maruyama with $\Delta t=0.02$ for 600 steps,
measured every 10 steps. The openness object is the normalized entropy
of the phase distribution over 10 bins, estimated from an ensemble of
20{,}000 replicates. The breakpoint contrast uses supercritical
$K=1.5$ against subcritical $K=0.3$; the coupling study spans
$K\in\{0.9,1.1,1.5,2.0,2.5\}$ with two seeds per coupling
(Supplementary Table~8).

\paragraph{Potts model.}
Ferromagnetic $q$-state Potts model ($q=2$ and $q=10$) on a $48\times
48$ periodic lattice with checkerboard Metropolis dynamics (energy
$-1$ per matching nearest-neighbour pair). Temperature is scanned
through multiples $\{1.35,\dots,0.70\}$ of the exact critical
temperature $T_c(q)=1/\ln(1+\sqrt{q})$, with 900 thermalization and
300 sampling sweeps per temperature and six replicas from hot and
cold starts. Openness is the normalized entropy of the colour-occupancy
distribution; the order parameter is $(q\,f_{\max}-1)/(q-1)$ with
$f_{\max}$ the dominant-colour fraction. Hysteresis is measured as
the hot/cold-start openness gap.

\paragraph{Vicsek model.}
Standard Vicsek dynamics in a periodic box at density $\rho=2.0$:
each particle moves at speed $0.03$ and adopts the mean heading of
all particles within radius $1.0$ plus uniform angular noise on
$[-\eta/2,\eta/2]$. The noise is annealed from $\eta=3.0$ down to
$0.1$ (nine levels, 300 steps per level), with population sizes
$N\in\{100,\dots,3200\}$ and three replicas. Openness is the
normalized entropy of the heading distribution over 24 bins; the
order parameter is $\varphi=|\langle e^{i\theta}\rangle|$.

\paragraph{Swift--Hohenberg pattern selection.}
One-dimensional Swift--Hohenberg equation
$\partial_t u=ru-(1+\partial_x^2)^2u-u^3$ plus additive noise of
amplitude $10^{-3}$, integrated pseudospectrally (linear operator
implicit, cubic term explicit) on $M=256$ modes over a domain of
length $64\pi$ with $\Delta t=0.5$ for 500 steps and 20 replicas.
The pattern-forming case $r=0.2$ is contrasted with the stable case
$r=-0.2$. Openness is the normalized spectral entropy of the Fourier
power distribution; commitment concentrates power in the critical
band $|k-1|\leq 0.1$.

\paragraph{Schelling segregation.}
Two agent types occupy 45\% of a $50\times 50$ periodic lattice each,
with 10\% vacancies. An agent is unhappy when its same-type fraction
among occupied Moore neighbours falls below the tolerance
$\tau=0.35$; unhappy agents move to random vacancies. Dynamics run
for 300 sweeps (20 replicas, states saved every 5 sweeps), with a
$\tau=0$ control in which no agent is ever unhappy. Openness is the
normalized entropy of the same-type neighbour-count distribution.

\paragraph{Baseline predictors in the intervention race.}
The predictor race of Fig.~5b compares, for identical intervention
episodes, the switch-prediction AUC of five per-episode quantities:
the side-openness of the joint policy (the possibility-space object),
the object's position magnitude $|x|$, its velocity magnitude $|v|$,
the population attachment (grip) level, and the intervention time
index $\tau$ itself. All predictors are computed from the same stored
rollouts; AUCs are reported per seed and pooled.

\section*{Supplementary Note 1: registered misses and amendments}

Every experiment in this study was preceded by an internal frozen
protocol naming its prediction and falsification clause. This note
collects, in one place, every registered miss and every amendment,
each of which is also reported at its point of use in the main text
or Methods. The corresponding protocol files are released verbatim
with the code and raw outputs.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Detector amendments.} The effect-size gate and the
saturation-truncated analysis window were added after two
preregistered control failures (a flat subcritical false positive and
a saturation-knee capture) and re-validated on fresh seeds before any
headline adjudication.
\item \textbf{Retired instrument extension.} A settled-openness
envelope intended to certify non-monotone commitment kept false
positives below 0.05 in every validation round but never exceeded
0.71 power against its required 0.90, and was closed under its own
preregistered stopping rule without being applied to system data.
This is why the coordination-ring onset count is reported at 1/8.
\item \textbf{Semi-synthetic injection near-miss.} Recovery power for
commitments injected into the ring pipeline's own estimator saturated
at 0.88 against a registered threshold of 0.90; the 30-episode
evaluation budget caps single-curve power, and the result is recorded
as a near-miss.
\item \textbf{A2C partial replication.} The strict parity-thinning
clause passed in 3/5 advantage actor--critic seeds, failing solely on
thinned-subsample detector power; the threshold was not adjusted.
\item \textbf{Representation-equivalence bar missed.} The registered
$\geq$90\% verdict-preservation bar was missed (8/12 cells); per the
registered clause the measured equivalence class is reported instead
of blanket invariance (Supplementary Table~3).
\item \textbf{Initialization-sweep midpoint.} The initialization-scale
sweep moved commitment time monotonically in the signalling system as
predicted, but the role-system midpoint was non-monotone---a
registered miss reported as such.
\item \textbf{Barrier threshold calibration.} The convention
deviation-cost clause was registered at 0.50 where the payoff
structure caps the measurable cost at $0.4\times$ the
within-population payoff for five-symbol permutation codes; the
measured value 0.3996 sits at that structural ceiling and the
mis-calibrated clause is reported verbatim.
\item \textbf{Source-attribution relocation.} One registered audit
prediction on the decomposition ladder missed: under strong knob
interaction (pairwise copying beneath a parity mechanism) realized
structure genuinely relocates to the individual level, which the
ladder reports; this is a property of measuring realized structure,
not generator labels.
\item \textbf{Grid-resolution amendment.} At the original 25-update
grid the neural-replication onsets were under-resolved (1/7 and
1/10); a measurement-density amendment to 5-update resolution,
justified by the detector's validated grid-power curve, was
registered before the rerun, and both grids are reported.
\item \textbf{Training failure.} A preregistered attempt on the
unmodified MPE \texttt{simple\_spread} benchmark did not meet its
frozen competence precondition (conflict-episode coverage 3\% after
3{,}000 PPO updates) and is recorded as a training failure with no
theory claim made either way.
\item \textbf{Numerical defect.} One float32 entropy clamp that
rounded to a degenerate value was found and fixed in code; both
affected experiments were rerun with identical seeds and the
corrupted outputs archived.
\end{enumerate}
